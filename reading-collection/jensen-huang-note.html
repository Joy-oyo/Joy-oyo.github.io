Adapt the car to you

You don’t have to change your personality or behavior when you become a CEO. You can continue to be yourself.

One thing I learned a long time ago—I have no idea where I first came across it—is this: as a CEO and founder, you’re building a car that you are going to race. You want to build a Formula 1 car, but you have to build it in a way that you can drive it. Adapt the car to you.

— Jensen Huang

SourceJensen Huang, founder & CEO, NVIDIA
SubjectLeadership, self-fit, organization design
FormPaper note — a quotation kept, not a book summarized

Why it’s hereFit runs both ways. When the machine will not change, change the machine — design the role, the team, and the process so the person driving them can actually drive them.
